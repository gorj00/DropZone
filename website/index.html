How it works

Earn your screen time

Pick the apps that steal your day. DropZone keeps them locked until you've done what moves you toward your goal.

Set your goal

Choose to lose weight or build muscle. Your goal is split into milestones, each with a real reward you pick.

Clear daily quests

Healthy meals, steps, weigh-ins and workouts. Prove each one with a photo, Health Connect or the camera rep counter.

Unlock your apps

Every cleared quest unlocks your doomscrolling apps for a while. Skip the quests and they stay locked.

Two paths

One app, your direction

Lose weight

Weigh-ins, healthy meals and daily steps, with milestones on the way down to your target weight.

Build muscle

Workouts counted by your camera, protein-friendly meals and progress milestones on the way up.
