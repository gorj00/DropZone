1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is J. Gorol, a sole trader based in the Czech Republic (EU) (“we”, “us”). Contact: info@dropzone.mobi.

2. What we collect and why

The legal basis for most processing is our contract with you: we need the data to provide the features you use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Where we rely on something else, it is named below.

Account

  • From Google sign-in: your name, e-mail address, profile picture and Google account ID.
  • What you set: your handle, display name, bio, avatar, theme and app settings, and your phone’s time zone.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted and when. We keep this to show we have your agreement (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • Your choice about anonymised statistics, when you made it, and how we determined whether you are in the EU (see section 8).

Goals and progress

  • Your path (lose weight or build muscle), goals, start and target values, weight, body measurements and lifts, milestones, rewards and deadlines.
  • Daily quests, check-ins, results, streaks, slip-ups and the notes you add to them.
  • This data is about your body. You choose to enter it, and we use it to run DropZone for you and, only in anonymised form, as described in section 8.

Photos

  • Proof photos, before and after photos, reward pictures and avatars you upload, together with the labels and numbers your phone recognised in them (see section 3).

Friends and community

  • Friends, friend requests and invite links, posts, comments, hypes, proof verifications, leaderboard entries, reports and blocks.
  • Notifications we show you in the app (for example friend requests, comments and reminders).

Purchases

  • Your trial dates, PRO status, plan and renewal dates, received from Google Play through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.

Device and technical data

  • The list of apps you chose to lock (their package names).
  • A push token, so we can send notifications you allowed.
  • Crash reports: app version, phone model, Android version, the error and your internal DropZone user ID, so we can fix bugs (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Server logs: IP address, time, the request and your user ID, to keep DropZone secure and working (legitimate interest).

Support

  • If you write to us, your e-mail and what you send, to answer you.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not use an advertising ID, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

3. What stays on your phone

Photo checks, pose detection and reading your scale run on your phone with Google ML Kit. Your photos are not sent anywhere for that. Only the result (for example the labels found in a photo or the number read from the scale) is saved with your proof.

4. Health Connect

If you allow it, DropZone reads your steps and sleep from Health Connect to complete your daily step and sleep quests automatically. We store only the daily values needed for your quests. DropZone does not write to Health Connect and reads no other data types. Health Connect data is never included in anonymised statistics. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Health Connect (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).

DropZone’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it except as needed to run DropZone.

5. App lock and Accessibility

The app lock uses Android’s Accessibility service only to see which app is open, so it can lock the apps you chose until you finish your quests. This happens on your phone. DropZone does not read the content of other apps, does not record what you type, and does not send your app usage to us. Only the list of apps you chose to lock is saved to your account. You turn the service on yourself and can turn it off in Android settings at any time.

6. Who can see what

  • Your goals, measurements and photos are private unless you share them. For each milestone and proof you choose private, friends or everyone.
  • Friends see what you share with friends. Everyone means all DropZone users.
  • Other users can find you by your handle or name, unless you turn on Hide me from search. You appear on friends’ leaderboards unless you turn on Hide me from leaderboards.
  • When your PRO ends, your shared posts are hidden until you subscribe again.
  • Shared content can be seen and saved by the people you share it with. Share only what you are comfortable with.

7. Automated checks

Photos you share publicly are checked by Google Cloud Vision for nudity and sexual content. Flagged photos are hidden until we review them (legitimate interest in keeping the community safe, Art. 6(1)(f)). Accounts with repeated removals are suspended automatically. If you disagree with a decision, write to us and a person will review it. We make no other automated decisions about you.

8. Improving DropZone and anonymised statistics

Improving DropZone

We analyse how DropZone is used, for example which quests people complete and where they stop, to fix problems and improve the app. This stays with us (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by writing to us.

Anonymised statistics

  • We may create statistics from goals, weight and body measurement changes, quests, streaks and app usage, combined from many users so that no person can be identified.
  • They never include photos, comments, notes, names, contact details or Health Connect data.
  • We may share or sell these statistics to third parties, such as research organisations, health and fitness companies, analytics and market research companies and marketing partners.
  • Anonymised statistics are not personal data, so they may be kept after you delete your account.

Your choice in the EU

If you are in the European Union, your data is only used for anonymised statistics if you opt in (your consent, Art. 6(1)(a)). You choose when you accept the Terms and can change it any time in Settings → Share anonymised statistics. If you opt out, your data is left out of new statistics. Outside the EU, this use is part of our Terms.

To decide whether you are in the EU, the app checks the country of your SIM card, then of your mobile network, then your phone’s region setting, and finally its time zone. We save which of these was used, what it showed and the result, so we can show why you were or were not offered the choice.

9. Service providers

We use these providers to run DropZone. They process data on our behalf or as independent providers of their own service:

  • Google Cloud (Google Ireland Ltd.): servers, database, photo storage, logs and the image check, in the EU (Netherlands).
  • Google Firebase: push notifications (Cloud Messaging) and crash reports (Crashlytics).
  • Google sign-in and Google Play: signing in, payments and subscriptions.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): subscription status, linked to your internal DropZone user ID only.
  • Unsplash (USA): when you search for reward pictures, our server sends your search words to Unsplash. Unsplash pictures load directly from Unsplash, so Unsplash sees your IP address.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: delivers this website and sees visitors’ IP addresses.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users and of DropZone.

10. Where data is stored

Our database and photos are stored in the EU (Google Cloud, Netherlands). Some providers above, such as RevenueCat, Unsplash, Cloudflare and Google, may process data in the USA or other countries. Such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Inactive accounts: deleted after 24 months without use, after a warning 30 days before.
  • Photos: removed from storage as soon as you delete them or your account.
  • In-app notifications: 180 days.
  • Database backups: up to 7 days, then overwritten.
  • Server logs: 30 days. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records at Google Play and RevenueCat: as their policies and tax law require. Deleting your account also deletes your RevenueCat record.
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to handle your request.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before the withdrawal).

  • Download your data: Settings → Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account, or dropzone.mobi/delete-account.
  • Anything else: write to info@dropzone.mobi. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live. In the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz); in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

13. US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to get a copy, to correct and delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. The categories we collect are listed in section 2, and the purposes and providers in sections 2, 8 and 9. Anonymised statistics (section 8) cannot identify you and are not personal information. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive information to infer characteristics about you. To use your rights, use the options in section 12.

14. Age limit

DropZone is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete the account.

15. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links to the people allowed to see them. Access to our systems is limited and protected. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

16. This website

dropzone.mobi uses no cookies for tracking and no analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address. The account deletion page uses Google sign-in to confirm it is you.

17. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change matters to you, we will tell you in the app before it applies and, where needed, ask for your agreement. The version and date are at the top of this page.

18. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: info@dropzone.mobi.
