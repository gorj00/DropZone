How it works

Four steps to your pass

It takes a couple of minutes to join. After that, just use DropZone like you normally would and tell us what you think.

Join the Discord

Our Discord is where testers get the app, report bugs and share ideas.

DM your Gmail

On Discord, send a direct message to the server owner (the name with the crown in the member list) with your Gmail address, the Google account you use for Google Play on your Android phone.

Install the app

We add you to the test and send you a link. Open it on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install DropZone from Google Play.

Test for 14 days

Use DropZone, stay in the test for 14 days and post your feedback and bugs in the Discord.

FAQ

Good to know

Who can join?

Anyone with an Android phone and a Google account.

Do I pay anything?

No. Don't buy PRO during the test: purchases in the test are real, and you get PRO free anyway.

What if more than 20 people sign up?

Places go in the order we get your Gmail address. When 20 places are filled, the PRO offer closes.

When do I get PRO?

When your 14 days are done, we turn PRO on for the DropZone account you tested with.

What do you do with my Gmail address?

We use it only to add you to the test on Google Play.

What if I leave the test early?

Leaving the test before your 14 days are done ends the PRO offer for you.

Grab one of 20 places

Join the Discord and DM the server owner your Gmail address. We'll take it from there.
