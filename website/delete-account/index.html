Delete your DropZone account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, or here by signing in with the same Google account you use in DropZone.

Deleting removes your account and everything in it: your profile, goals, milestones, proofs and photos, posts, comments, friends and notifications. This can't be undone.

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
